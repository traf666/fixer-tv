## 📺 About

**Fixer TV** brings video from several sources into one TV-friendly app. Catalog, search, episodes, voice-overs and
bookmarks — all controlled with the remote. No registration needed.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Compatibility** | Android 7.0 or newer — Android TV, Google TV, TV boxes |
| **Architecture** | `arm64-v8a` (64-bit) and `armeabi-v7a` (32-bit) |
| **Languages** | Russian and English |
| **Price** | free, no ads, every feature open to everyone |

## ✨ Features

- 🎬 **Catalog** — new releases, movies, series, cartoons and anime; filters and sorting; posters, descriptions and ratings.
- 🔎 **Search** by title.
- 🔀 **Several video sources** — quality and number of voice-overs at a glance; if a video does not start, the app switches to the next source by itself.
- 📂 **Seasons, episodes and voice-overs** — episode sorting, watched marks.
- ⏯️ **Continue watching, Bookmarks, New episodes** — resume where you stopped, your own bookmark folders, new episodes of your series.
- ⬇️ **Downloads** — save a movie or an episode and watch offline.
- 🧲 **Torrents** — releases right on the movie page.
- 🧒 **Kids mode** with a PIN and hidden countries.
- 📱 **Web panel** — settings from your phone on the home network.
- 💾 **Backup** of history, bookmarks and settings — to a folder or a USB drive.
- 👥 **Several accounts**, each with its own settings.
- 🔄 **Updates itself** — every update is checked by signature and cannot be replaced.

## 📥 Installation

1. Pick the file for your device: **64-bit** for most modern boxes, **32-bit** for Mi Box, sticks and older models.
   Not sure — try 64-bit; if Android says the app is not compatible, take 32-bit.
2. Copy the file to the TV box in any way you like:
   - with the **Downloader** app right on the TV;
   - from your phone with **Send Files to TV**;
   - from a USB drive.
3. Open the file and allow installing from unknown sources if Android asks.
4. Accept the license agreement on first launch — and enjoy.

**Updates:** Settings → About → App update. The app downloads the new version from this page and checks the file
signature and checksum before installing.
