<a id="why"></a>

## 🎯 Why this app

I made Fixer TV first of all for myself and my family. The goal is to gather as many free, publicly available video
sources in different quality as possible in one app. Then there is no need to hunt for movies across sites and browsers
and sit through their ads.

- **Free and without ads.** There are no ads in the app, and I do not plan to add any.
- **Donations are optional.** The big «Support the project» button is for those who can and want to help. It is a
  voluntary crypto transfer, not a purchase: it unlocks nothing, and the app works the same without it.
- **I am not abandoning the app.** I fix bugs and release updates when time allows. I promise no deadlines or new features.

<a id="advantages"></a>

## 🏆 Advantages

- 📌 **Remembers everything you watched — in any source.** Season, episode, the minute you stopped and «watched» marks are
  kept on the TV box and tied to the movie, not to a site: start in one source, continue in another or in a torrent — from
  the same moment. A source closed or went paid — your history stays. Backup included.
- 🔔 **Tells you about new episodes by itself.** The app checks series from your bookmarks, plus the ones you started or
  finished, on its own — each one every hour. When a new episode is out and a source already has it with a voice-over, the
  series appears in the «Updates» section of the app and in the «Updates» row on the Android TV home screen. Nothing to
  look up or check by hand.
- **Completely free.** No ads, no subscriptions, no paid tiers — everything is open to everyone.
- **Switches sources by itself.** If a video does not start or stops midway, the app continues in the next source from
  the same moment. Nothing to pick by hand. Once a day the app checks the sources on fresh films and episodes and measures
  how fast the video goes — slow ones are tried after fast ones.
- **Torrents with no setup.** TorrServer is built in and starts by itself — no separate app to install. About 5 minutes
  of video ahead are buffered on the device storage, sized by the file size and film length: seeking inside the buffer
  is almost instant, and the buffer is deleted after watching. Have your own TorrServer — enter its address; if it does not
  answer, the app switches back to the built-in one.
- **Control from your phone.** A web panel on the home network: sign-in, all settings, the log and backups — opened by a
  QR code on the screen.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **A profile for everyone in the family.** Each has its own watch history, bookmarks, new episodes and settings.
  A kids profile has an allowed age, a parent PIN and a daily time limit.
- **Protected updates.** Every release is signed: the app checks the signature and the checksum and will not install a
  replaced file.

## ✨ Features

Tap an item to open its description with a screenshot and the path in the app.

- 🎬 [**Catalog**](#f-catalog) — new releases, movies, series, cartoons and anime; filters (including any set of years) and sorting.
- 🔎 [**Search**](#f-search) by title, with suggestions and recent queries; you can type from your phone via QR.
- 🎞️ [**Movie page**](#f-page) — description, rating, cast, franchise parts, trailer, bookmarks.
- 🔀 [**Several video sources**](#f-sources) — quality and number of voice-overs at a glance; if one fails, the app takes the next.
- 📂 [**Seasons, episodes and voice-overs**](#f-episodes) — episode sorting, watched marks, voice-over shares.
- ▶️ [**Player**](#f-player) — voice-over and source right in the player, subtitles, speed up to 1.75×, volume boost, zoom, sleep timer, intro skip.
- ⏯️ [**Continue watching, Bookmarks, Updates**](#f-continue) — resume where you stopped, your own folders, new episodes.
- ⬇️ [**Downloads**](#f-downloads) — save a movie or an episode and watch offline.
- 🧲 [**Torrents**](#f-torrents) — releases right on the movie page, filter by quality, size and voice-over; built-in or your own TorrServer.
- 🧒 [**Kids mode**](#f-kids) with a PIN, kids profiles with a time limit, hidden countries.
- 👥 [**Profiles**](#f-profiles) — for everyone in the family: own history, bookmarks and settings.
- 🎨 [**Appearance**](#f-look) — 5 themes, highlight color, three text sizes, number of columns, captions on the poster or below it, language.
- 📱 [**Web panel**](#f-panel) — settings from your phone on the home network.
- 💾 [**Backup**](#f-backup) of history, bookmarks and settings — to a folder or a USB drive.
- 🔄 [**Updates itself**](#f-update) — every update is checked by signature.

<a id="f-torrents"></a>
<details>
<summary><b>🧲 Torrents</b></summary>

Releases appear as the «Torrents» row in the «Sources» window. TorrServer is built into the app and starts by itself.
A few minutes of video ahead are buffered (on disk or in a folder — 5 to 30 min, your choice); you choose where: on the device storage, in memory or in your own folder
(for example, a flash drive), with a 1–8 GB limit; the buffer is deleted after watching. Subtitles from the release (.srt, .ass) are added by themselves. The «Torrents» window has a
filter by quality, size and voice-over. You can set your own TorrServer and your own torrent search (Jackett or
JacRed) — the lists are merged without duplicates. Releases are searched on all search sites at once, by the film's
number and by title; those without Russian audio go to the end. While a release sends its file list, the wait progress is
shown; if it fails, the app suggests a neighbouring one.
**Where:** Movie page → Source → Torrents · Settings → Torrents.

## 📥 Installation

1. Pick the file for your device: **64-bit** for most modern boxes, **32-bit** for Mi Box, sticks and older models.
   Not sure — try 64-bit; if Android says the app is not compatible, take 32-bit.
2. Copy the file to the TV box in any way you like:
   - with the **Downloader** app right on the TV;
   - from your phone with **Send Files to TV**;
   - from a USB drive.
3. Open the file and allow installing from unknown sources if Android asks.
4. On first launch, accept the license agreement and create a profile — and enjoy.

**Why the app asks for permissions:**
- **Internet and network state** — movies, the catalog, updates; the log notes when the network dropped.
- **Install apps** — only for its own update (Settings → About); the file is checked before installing.
- **All files access** (Android 11+; on Android 7–10, storage write) — backups to a folder you choose and to a USB
  drive, finding your old backups after reinstalling. Without it, backups stay in the app's own storage.
- **Background work, start on boot, notifications** — the web panel, downloads and new-episode checks keep working while
  the TV shows something else; Android requires a notification icon for this.
- **Read system logs** — never granted by itself, only with `adb shell pm grant reshala.tv.app android.permission.READ_LOGS`.
  Needed only to investigate sound problems: decoder messages go into the debug log. Everything works without it.

**Questions and bugs** — in the [app thread on 4PDA](https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=1127834).

**Updates:** Settings → About → App update. The app downloads the new version from this page and checks the file
signature and checksum before installing.
