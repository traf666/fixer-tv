<a id="why"></a>

## 🎯 Why this app

I made Fixer TV first of all for myself and my family. The goal is to gather as many free, publicly available video
sources in different quality as possible in one app. Then there is no need to hunt for movies across sites and browsers
and sit through their ads.

- **Free and without ads.** There are no ads in the app, and I do not plan to add any.
- **Donations are optional.** The big «Support the project» button is for those who can and want to help. It is a
  voluntary crypto transfer, not a purchase: it unlocks nothing, and the app works the same without it.
- **I am not abandoning the app.** I fix bugs and release updates when time allows. I promise no deadlines or new features.

<a id="advantages"></a>

## 🏆 Advantages

- 📌 **Remembers everything you watched — in any source.** Season, episode, the minute you stopped and «watched» marks are
  kept on the TV box and tied to the movie, not to a site: start in one source, continue in another or in a torrent — from
  the same moment. A source closed or went paid — your history stays. Backup included.
- **Completely free.** No ads, no subscriptions, no paid tiers — everything is open to everyone.
- **Switches sources by itself.** If a video does not start or stops midway, the app continues in the next source from
  the same moment. Nothing to pick by hand.
- **Torrents with no setup.** TorrServer is built in and starts by itself — no separate app to install.
- **Control from your phone.** A web panel on the home network: sign-in, all settings, the log and backups — opened by a
  QR code on the screen.
- **Kids mode with a PIN and several profiles**, each with its own settings.
- **Protected updates.** Every release is signed: the app checks the signature and the checksum and will not install a
  replaced file.

## ✨ Features

<a id="install"></a>

## 📥 Installation

1. Pick the file for your device: **64-bit** for most modern boxes, **32-bit** for Mi Box, sticks and older models.
   Not sure — try 64-bit; if Android says the app is not compatible, take 32-bit.
2. Copy the file to the TV box in any way you like:
   - with the **Downloader** app right on the TV;
   - from your phone with **Send Files to TV**;
   - from a USB drive.
3. Open the file and allow installing from unknown sources if Android asks.
4. Accept the license agreement on first launch — and enjoy.

**Questions and bugs** — in the app thread on 4PDA (will open on release day).

**Updates:** Settings → About → App update. The app downloads the new version from this page and checks the file
signature and checksum before installing.
